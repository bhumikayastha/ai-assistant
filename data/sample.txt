A transformer is a deep learning neural network architecture introduced in the 2017 paper ["Attention Is All You Need"] that processes entire data sequences simultaneously rather than word-by-word. By utilizing a "self-attention" mechanism, it dynamically weighs the contextual relationships between different parts of the input data, enabling modern generative AI systems like [ChatGPT] and [Gemini] to understand nuance, process vast datasets in parallel, and generate human-like text
